Screenshot of the reference product's third gene page, "III. Filter and effects"
(captured 2026-10-22). See the provenance note in the design document: where
this page and the reference's written documentation disagree, the documentation
wins.

Top left, the filter group: flt_type, flt_q, flt_mod, flt_sep, flt_freq, flt_kf.
flt_type is drawn as a small selector rather than a knob, which is the visual
form of a stepped, topology selecting gene (design document 5.7.5) and the reason
its value crosses with a crossfade and not a slew. Next to the group is
"figure 3a Filter response curve": a flat passband with a gentle rise at the top,
i.e. a resonance limited enough never to self oscillate.

Below it, a single control, saturate, drawn in the page's warm colour, with a
small S-shaped curve beside it - the drive curve, soft at the bottom and bending
over. Its placement on the page (with the filter group, before the effects) is
one side of the open question 5 in section 15, which asks whether saturation is
per voice here and the master clipper is adj_clip, or the other way round.

Left, the reverb group: rvb_mix, rvb_atk, rvb_len, rvb_damp, rvb_chor, rvb_size,
next to "figure 3b Artificial reverberation": a burst of reflections decaying
into a smooth tail, drawn several times over, which is the "chorusing reverb"
the reference's text describes (taps inside the tail, not a chorus in front of
it).

Bottom, the output adjusters: adj_bass and adj_treb on the left, adj_pan and
adj_clip on the right, the last one marked with an asterisk, and a flat bar
between them where a frequency response plot would go. These four, with
rvb_size, are the growth exempt set of design document 5.7.6: they belong to the
player and do not move when a branch grows.

The asterisks on adj_clip (here) and lfo_amt (page I) are unexplained; see open
question 2 of section 15. GenoMosh gives asterisked genes no special behaviour.
