Screenshot of the reference product's second gene page, "II. Oscillators"
(captured 2026-10-22). See the provenance note in the design document: where
this page and the reference's written documentation disagree, the documentation
wins.

Left column, oscillator A: a_form, a_noise, a_mod, a_color, a_freq, next to
"figure 2a Oscillator A" - a single clean cycle, i.e. the page shows A on its
own, before anything modulates it.

Middle, the FM/mix group: fm_mod, fm_amt on one row and mix_mod, osc_mix on the
next, with arrows drawn from them down into A and up into B. This is the
diagram that places the FM and mix controls between the two oscillators rather
than after them.

Left column, oscillator B: b_form, b_noise, b_mod, sub_am, b_freq, b_sh, next
to "figure 2b Oscillator B" - a pulse-shaped wave at roughly the same period as
figure 2a, which is how B reads as a relative of A rather than a second
unrelated source.

The arrows drawn between the two figures connect them into one chain: a green
arrow runs from figure 2a down into the fm_mod / fm_amt / mix_mod / osc_mix row,
and a line continues from that row into figure 2b. Read as a diagram, A and B and
their FM/mix controls are one signal path, not two independent sources - which is
how the reference presents "A modulated by B" as the default direction.

No control on this page is a waveform duty/PWM control, which is the origin of
open question 4 in section 15 (assume the pulse width is fixed).
