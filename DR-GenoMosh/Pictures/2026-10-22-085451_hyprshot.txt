Screenshot of the reference product's first gene page, "I. Envelope and LFO"
(captured 2026-10-22). Revision 2 of the design document was written from these
three pages; see the provenance note in the design document section 1/2. Where a
screenshot and the reference's own written documentation disagree, the
documentation wins.

Top row, the shared envelope clock: env_time, env_loop, env_tilt, env_kf.
The knobs are greyed at their neutral position and the arc is drawn from it.

Left column, the volume group: vol_atk, vol_dcy, vol_sus, vol_fade, next to
"figure 1a Volume envelope" - a repeating envelope whose attack is steep and
whose decay curves down to a floor, i.e. duration and repeat drawn as one clock.

Left column, the modulation group: mod_atk, mod_dcy, mod_sh, mod_vel, next to
"figure 1b Modulation envelope" - the same shape driving parameters instead of
level, each cycle cut short at the bottom.

Left column, the LFO group: lfo_rate, lfo_amt (marked with an asterisk),
lfo_bal, lfo_dly, next to "figure 1c Pitch oscillation (vibrato)" and
"figure 1d Volume oscillation (tremolo)" - two waveforms of identical
frequency, amplitude-modulated and frequency-modulated at once, which is what
tells us one LFO drives both destinations at one rate.

The asterisk on lfo_amt is unexplained (open question 2 of section 15). Nothing
in this page gives it a distinct behaviour, so GenoMosh gives it none; the
documented growth-exempt set is implemented as kFlagNever regardless.

This page is the source of the 16 genes of design document 4.2 group 1
(4 + 4 + 4 + 4) and of the three-page grouping the UI is expected to follow
(section 12).
